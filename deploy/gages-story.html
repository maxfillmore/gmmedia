Where it started

I didn't start GM Media because I was obsessed with marketing.

I started it because I became obsessed with understanding human behavior — what captures someone's attention, what builds trust, and ultimately what drives someone to buy a product or service.

The more I studied content, psychology, and consumer behavior, the more I realized that attention alone isn't enough.

Anyone can get views. The real challenge is turning attention into trust, and trust into action.

That obsession led me to study not just what makes content perform, but why people connect with certain brands, remember certain stories, and choose one business over another.

The gap I found in dental

Most agencies expected doctors to film it themselves.

They'd hand over a content calendar, ask the team to record videos on their phones, and hope for the best. It was convenient for the agency — but it wasn't creating exceptional content.

I believed there was a better way. Instead of asking busy doctors to become content creators, I built a creative team that travels directly to their practices, handles the entire production process, and creates content at a level the industry hadn't seen before.

The goal was simple: make social media a true growth asset without taking doctors away from what they do best — treating patients.

Our crew, in the practice, doing the work.
The formula

Miss any one of the three and the content stalls. Most agencies only ever solve for the first.

Attention + Trust + Drive = Growth
Attention

The first part of the equation is capturing their attention. If they don't stop the scroll, nothing else matters.

Trust

Showing competence and empathy in your content, so the viewer knows you genuinely care about them.

Drive

Understanding what actually motivates a patient to book — and speaking to that, not to the algorithm.
