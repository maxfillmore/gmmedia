The real problem

Most dentists don't know how to create content that actually connects.

It isn't a lack of effort. You post the office tour, the new equipment, the before-and-after — and it lands with the people who already know you. Nobody new stops scrolling, because nothing in it was made for them.

Connecting is a different skill than dentistry. It takes knowing what makes a stranger stop, what makes them trust a doctor they've never met, and what actually moves them to book. That's a craft — and it's not the one you spent a decade mastering.

So the average practice that knows how to connect beats the great practice that doesn't.
